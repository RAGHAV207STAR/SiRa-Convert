About SiRa Convert

SiRa Convert is a browser-first toolkit for everyday document jobs: convert images to PDF, extract pages as JPG/PNG, and merge PDFs. The product is designed to stay fast, easy, and practical on both mobile and desktop.

3 Core Tools JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Merge PDF
Private Flow Core processing is designed for in-browser use
PWA Ready Installable app with offline shell support
Cross Device Built for Android, iOS, desktop, and tablets

Mission

Reduce document friction. Every release focuses on fewer steps, reliable output, and clear controls for non-technical users.

How We Design
