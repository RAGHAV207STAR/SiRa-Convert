1. Tool-Specific Processing

  • JPG to PDF: Selected images are assembled into PDF output.
  • PDF to JPG/PNG: Chosen pages are rendered to selected image format.
  • Merge PDF: File queue is combined in user-defined order.
  • Photo Compressor: Images are compressed to target sizes using in-browser processing; optional ZIP export bundles outputs.

2. Browser-First Design

The app architecture prioritizes client-side handling for core workflows to reduce unnecessary transfer of sensitive content.

3. Analytics

We use analytics tools (including Google Analytics) to understand usage trends, detect reliability issues, and prioritize improvements.

4. Advertising and Cookies

Ads may be served using Google AdSense and related partners. These services can use cookies, local storage, and identifiers for ad measurement and quality controls.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Hosting/infrastructure providers may process standard network logs.
  • Authentication providers may apply their own privacy terms if sign-in is used.
  • Third-party services are governed by their own policies.

6. User Controls

  • Clear browser cache/storage when needed.
  • Manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings.
  • Use browser privacy settings to limit cookies and trackers.

7. Security and Responsibility

We maintain reasonable technical safeguards, but you should also secure your device and keep backups of important exported files.

8. Policy Updates

Privacy terms may be revised as laws, infrastructure, or product capabilities evolve. Updated dates will be shown on this page.
