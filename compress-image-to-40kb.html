How to Compress Image to Exact KB Online

Follow these simple steps to compress any photo to 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, or your own target size.

  1. Step 1: Upload your image

    Click “Upload Images” or drag and drop JPG, PNG, or WEBP files into the box. You can add up to 50 photos in one batch.

  2. Step 2: Enter the target size in KB

    Type an exact size like 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB, or any custom value. The tool aims to reach or stay just under that number.

  3. Step 3: Click compress

    Press “Compress All Images.” Each file is processed in your browser with our fast algorithm to shrink photos without unnecessary quality loss.

  4. Step 4: Download the compressed image

    Save files individually or download everything as a ZIP. Perfect for online forms, college portals, and passport uploads.
