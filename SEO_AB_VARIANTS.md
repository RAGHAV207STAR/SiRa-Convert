## How to test
1. Run one variant for 14-28 days.
2. In Search Console, compare by page:
   - `Clicks`
   - `Impressions`
   - `CTR`
   - `Average position`
3. Keep the winner and move to next variant.
4. Re-request indexing for updated URLs after each variant swap.

## Notes
- Change only title/meta text during one test cycle.
- Keep canonical URL unchanged.
- Avoid testing during major seasonality spikes when possible.
